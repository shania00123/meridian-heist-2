The Meridian Heist: a top-down stealth heist game — slip past guards and sweeping cameras, crack the loot case, and get out before the alarm hits 100%. Three floors of the Meridian await: the Gallery (the $250,000 Meridian Diamond), the Aurelia Vault (the $400,000 Aurelia Core behind a laser grid), and the Sky Penthouse (the $600,000 Sky Crown under four cameras). Guards patrol with amber vision cones and give chase when their suspicion fills; cameras sweep red cones that feed the alarm; blinking lasers trip it too. Hold E to crack a case (1.4 s) or hack a camera junction (2 s), click to toss one of three coins and lure a guard away, sneak with Shift, and mind the big ALARM meter — at 100% the Meridian locks down. Grab the loot, pocket the side loot, and reach the GETAWAY door. Best take is saved in your browser. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network request — plain static files under `game/`.
