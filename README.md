The Meridian Heist: a top-down stealth heist game — slip past guards and sweeping cameras, crack the loot case, and get out before the alarm hits 100%. Four floors of the Meridian await: the Gallery (the $250,000 Meridian Diamond), the Aurelia Vault (the $400,000 Aurelia Core behind a laser grid), the Sky Penthouse (the $600,000 Sky Crown under four cameras), and the Rooftop at dusk (the $750,000 Meridian Star, guarded by three guards and two cameras under the open sky). Guards patrol with amber vision cones and give chase when their suspicion fills — they run for where they last saw you, so smoke, vents and walls are real cover. Cameras sweep red cones that feed the alarm; blinking lasers trip it too. Hold E to crack a case (1.4 s) or hack a camera junction (2 s), click to toss one of three coins and lure a guard away, sneak with Shift, and mind the big ALARM meter — at 100% the Meridian locks down. Every room has a vent crawlspace (a second route where no cone can find you) and two tools at a cost: Q drops a smoke bomb (one per room) and F fires an EMP that kills every camera for 6 seconds (once per run, +10 alarm). Crack the case and the getaway begins: the alarm jumps, every guard runs for you, and the van waits 20 seconds — reach the GETAWAY door in time for a Perfect, Clean or Loud rank. Grab the loot, pocket the side loot, and reach the GETAWAY door. Best take is saved in your browser. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network request — plain static files under `game/`.
